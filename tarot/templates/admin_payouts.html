This ledger is the record, not a payment rail. The provider settles the full amount to one payee and does not split, so readers invoice and are paid separately. Marking paid out here records that it happened — it does not move money.

That makes this a studio, not a marketplace. The site is the seller, readers are subcontractors, and the site owes them for delivered work whether or not the customer later refunds. Readers working exclusively and to a fixed schedule can be reclassified as employees under German law — worth a look at how the arrangement is actually run, not just how it is written.
