What is my tarot birth card?

Tarot has no birth chart — every reading is drawn from the same 78 cards. The birth card is the one exception: a card the tradition assigns to you permanently, calculated from the date you were born.

Nothing is stored. The calculation happens on the page and your date is not saved.

Your card for {{ year }}

{{ result.year_card.name }}. {{ result.year_card.up }}

Unlike the birth card, this one moves. It is recalculated each year from your birthday and the current year, and it describes the season you are in rather than who you are.

How this is calculated

Add your month, day and full year together, then reduce the total by adding its digits until it is 22 or below — 22 folds back to The Fool at zero. If the result is a two-digit number, its own digit sum gives the soul card beneath it. This is the traditional method used by Angeles Arrien and Mary Greer; it is not something invented for this site.
