Why the year matters

The method adds month, day and year together, then reduces by digit sum. Because the year is part of the sum, two people born on the same day a year apart usually get different cards. That is the intended behaviour, not a flaw — the birth card is meant to be personal, and a date alone is shared by roughly a two-hundredth of everyone alive.
